# Quest Info

Strahd has bitten Ireena, and some Barovians fear Ireena is to blame for the recent attacks, due to her red hair. Ismark and Ireena both agree it isn't safe in the village for her, so they've asked you to escort Ireena to St. Andral's church in Vallaki.

After arriving in Vallaki, the party had to help Father Lucian in finding the bones of St Andral, to hallow the church.

- [x] Obtain the quest
- [x] Stop by [[Tser Pool Encampment]] to hear from Madam Eva
- [x] Escort Ireena safely to St. Andral's Church
