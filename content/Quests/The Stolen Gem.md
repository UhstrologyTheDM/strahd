# Quest Details

Date Obtained: Neyavr 10th
Status: In Progress
Quest Giver: [[Davian Martikov]]
Quest Location: [[The Wizard of Wines]]
Date Due: Neyavr 11th 
A raven delivered a note from [[Urwin Martikov]], who asked the party to investigate a mysterious shortage of wine deliveries from the Wizard of Wines winery to the southwest. Urwin asks them to allow Muriel Vinshaw, an employee of the winery, to accompany them on the journey. 

Before the players departed Vallaki, [[Ireena Kolyana]], inspired by the players’ exploits there and filled with grim determination following her encounters with Izek Strazni, asked them to allow her to accompany them on their travels, forsaking the sanctuary of Vallaki for an opportunity to join the fight against Strahd.

Upon arriving at the Wizard of Wines, the players met [[Davian Martikov]], the patriarch of the Martikov family, and his assorted children and grandchildren, who have taken shelter in a hidden cavern beneath the nearby woods. Davian shared that the winery has been invaded by a group of druids known as the Forest Folk alongside a horde of twisted plant creatures called **blights**. Davian asked the players to defeat the Forest Folk and break the staff that their leader uses to control the blights, allowing the Martikovs to reclaim their home.

To do so, the players entered the winery through a secret tunnel that lead into the cellar, then did battle with the druids and their corrupted blight servants. The druids, however, were nurturing a cutting of the “Gulthias tree"—a vampiric tree that grows atop Yester Hill to the south—within the walls of the winery, complicating the players’ efforts to reach the upper levels.

The players defeated the druids, and learned that the Forest Folk are planning a ritual to summon a being called “Wintersplinter" at Yester Hill the following day, a being that threatens to smash the winery into pieces. Davian asks the players and Muriel to journey to Yester Hill and prevent Wintersplinter’s birth. Davian also asks the players to retrieve an enchanted gemstone that the winery previously used to grow grapes before it was stolen by the druids.

At Yester Hill, Muriel first guided the players to a grove in the shadow of the Svalich Woods. There, she summoned the spirit of Kavan, an ancient chieftain of the Forest Folk, who provided information regarding the druids’ plans and defenses. Kavan warned the players that Svarog, the druids’ leader, is a mighty foe, and invited them to return to his cairn should they defeat Svarog and retrieve his yew wand.

As the players continued their journey to Yester Hill, they were met by Strahd von Zarovich riding his **nightmare**, Beucephalus. The players convinced Strahd to allow them to oppose the druids, Strahd suggested a wager and offered a reward should they win, which the players can collect at the Whispering Wall west of Yester Hill after the battle.

The players climbed Yester Hill, fought their way past the guardians of the stone ring atop the hill, and defeated Svarog’s circle of druids before they could summon Wintersplinter, a fearsome **tree blight** that threatened to lay siege to the Wizard of Wines winery.
