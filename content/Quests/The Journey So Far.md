# The Journey So Far


* During the night of the Harvest Festival in Daggerford, each of the PCs carried an item. Each of those items swept away to a small house surrounded by fog in the town, and the pcs followed and entered. 
* They found themselves in Death House, a haunted house that once belonged to the Durst family; a family that led a cult, and committed infanticide, twisting the horrible evil in the house into a living manifestation of the innocent child's souls anguish. The party eventually escaped, freeing the two kind souls living in the house and putting them to rest.
* Upon exiting, the party found themselves in a strange wood, surrounded by fog. They followed the path, eventually coming upon a dead horse and a rider. They followed the riders path into the woods, circling back onto the same tree thirteen times, eventuallly finding a compass in the fog and a carving of a rider, saying "the seer spoke true."
* The party continued on, eventually coming to the village of Barovia. There they met Ismark kolyanovich, Ireena Kolyana, and a host of Barovians. They were asked to help defend the village, as it was under siege from undead. The party stopped the siege, and were tasked with taking Ireena to the village of Vallaki, or Krezk, as she was being hunted by Strahd von Zarovich, and Vallaki and Krezk both have ground that could be hallowed.
* The party left the village of Barovia with Ireena.
* They encountered two barovian scouts on the road, and a group of 5 zombies - the family the scouts were looking for not far off the path. 
* Their they found Van Richten's stash, and laid the zombie family to rest.
* The party continued on, making it to the [[River Ivlis Crossroads]]. There they encountered Strahd. TCMS willingly went over to Strahd to protect Ireena.
* A lone Goliath Barbarian was fighting off a group of [[Strix]] that were attacking a raven with blue-tipped wings.
* The party made it to [[Tser Pool Encampment]], staying for the night. They had their fortunes read by [[Madam Eva]].
* The party departed early, encountering a skeletal rider on a horse. They then stumbled across a watchtower with 2 graves, and two slain wolves.
* The party made it to the Tser Falls Bridge, where they have encountered an Undead creature in Armor, standing in the middle of the bridge they need to pass. The creature revealed itself as a knight of the Silver Order, an order of knights who opposed Strahd in life, and to seek out Argynvostholt to hear more.
* The party continued towards Vallaki, and come to the base of a mountain upon which is perched a large stone windmill. An older woman comes into view, introducing herself as Morgantha, a peddler of baked wares to the good people of Barovia. She was returning from Vallaki, selling her pies to the people outside the gates. She lives in the old windmill on the hill with her daughters Bella and Offalia. She gave the party a few dream pastries - 'filled with the light and love of dreaming' - to the party, a free sample.
* After the encounter, the party continued on, entering into the dep woods. Here, dozens of ravens swarm around the party, following from a respectful distance, clearly shadowing the players.
* Upon coming into view of a lake, Ireena takes a quick detour, saying the lake seemed oddly familiar. She noted that appeared to be the same lake that she sees in her dreams.
* Continuing on, the forest pressed in close again, and the party got the sense it was being watched. They noticed a humanoid shadow in the distance that vanishes from sight, and they entered into a clearing where the party was ambushed by five wolves, two of which turned out to be werewolves.
* After the fight, and miraculously no one getting infected, the party moved on, now close to the town of Vallaki. They gained entry to the Gates of Vallaki, after showing their teeth ("to prove you aren't vampires" and being exposed to a necklace strung with garlic("to prove you aren't werewolves") and handing over any silver they have, for the town's guard to put to use in defense of the town.
* the players were left to explore the shadowed town of Vallaki, a Barovian settlement that has found itself embroiled in tension, tragedy, and intrigue in the wake of Strahd's awakening.
* As the players sought to complete lingering obligations; escorting Ireena to St. Andral's Church, and delivering a toy to the Vistani child Arabelle, they met new allies and enemies, including Victor Vallakovich, the son of Baron Vargas Vallakovich; Lady Fiona Wachter, the Baron's rival for power; Father Lucian Petrovich, the priest of St. Andral's Church; Luvash and Arrigal, the leaders of the Vallakian Vistani camp; and Dr. Rudolph van Richten, the veteran vampire hunter and lone survivor of Doru's rebellion.
* At St. Andral's Church, the players were tasked with recovering the stolen bones of St. Andral before the night of St. Andral's Feast in order to sustain the church's hallowed defenses. At the Vallakian Vistani encampment, the players were asked to find and rescue Arabelle following her mysterious disappearance.
* Soon after the players' arrival in Vallaki, Lady Wachter invited them to dine with her personally at her family estate, Wachterhaus, seeking to recruit them into a dangerous conspiracy. Meanwhile, Izek Strazni prowls Vallaki's streets for his long-lost sister, Ireena. And following a close encounter with the spirit of Erasmus van Richten, the players were tasked with obtaining a *heartstone* from the night hags of Old Bonegrinder - a mission that ultimately culminated in an ethereal combat with the vengeful ghost of Leo Dilisnya and the recovery of an ancient tome, one foretold by fate.
* The party retrieved the bones, discovered a wizard's tower at the nearby lake, journied to Argynvostholt and learned of the Order of the Silver Dragon, attempted to enter Krezk, rescued Arabelle, saved Stella Wachter's soul, saved Ireena from Izek, and attended the festival of the blazing sun, as Lady Wachter set her coup into place. They helped defeat Izek Strazni, and captured the Burgomaster, installing Lady Wachter as the Burgomaster of Vallaki. 
* The next day, they received a letter from Urwin asking them to help with a missing shipment of wine from the nearby Winery. While getting information about this quest, Rahadin, Strahd's chamberlain, enters the Blue Water Inn and hands the party an invitation to dinner at Castle Ravenloft on the 14th of Neyavr, from Strahd von Zarovich himself. The party accepted the invitation and Rahadin left.
* The party, and Ireena, now resolved to never be a bystander again, together with Muriel Vinshaw left Vallaki for the Wizard of Wines.
* The party encountered a few blights and a pit trap in the road. They then heard the call of  a greater strix, one of the mechanical birds created by Baba Lysaga. Throk called back, then a figure ushered them into the woods.
* The party followed, meeting Davian martikov and his family. They asked the party to save the winery, as it was under attack by evil druids, the forest folk. They are commanding an army of blights, and raising a Gulthias Tree, a vampiric tree used for evil. If they obliged, Davian would give them information that he promised would be helpful.
* The party cleared the winery, snapping the staff used by the main druid, destroying the blights and saving the winery. The party interrogated a few of the druids, learning of a ritual to bring about Wintersplinter at Yester Hill, a giant blight made from cuttings of the Gulthias Tree, made to smash the winery into pieces.
* Davian asked the party to intervene, saving the winery for the Barovian people. And a reward in kind. 
* The party stopped the ritual, and Strahd stepped in to save Ireena from the backlash of magical energy that was unleashed by stopping it.
