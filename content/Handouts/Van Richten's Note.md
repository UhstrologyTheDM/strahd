_Ez --_

_This journal is filled with a mixture of ciphers, fictional stories, and notes written in a curious shorthand that only its author employs. I have come to suspect that this author is Strahd himself, and that this is some sort of journal. I took it from his desk during my time within the castle, and his fury has been such that I have been driven into a mistrustful hiding so deep that I fear to contact even you. His spies are everywhere, and He can appear as anyone._

_I am confident in my mission, and doubt you will ever have cause to read this note. However, if I fail, only you will know where to find this tome. I fear to spend too much time with it, lest he scry me doing so -- I know he watches much in this land, and his spies are everywhere. You should take the same precautions should it fall into your hands._

_I have recorded my notes and progress so far._

_Your teacher,_

_R._
